HOW TO PLAY

👆 Use your INDEX FINGER to play

🖐 Show both hands in front of the camera

👉 Left hand → controls GREEN saber

👉 Right hand → controls BLUE saber

👆 Move your index finger to move the saber

📦 Blocks will come from top

➡️ Hit the blocks using your finger

🔼 Follow the arrow direction carefully

⬅️➡️⬆️⬇️ Move your finger in same direction

❌ Do NOT touch RED blocks

❤️ If you touch red → you lose life

🤏 Pinch (thumb + index) to START game

🎯 Keep playing and get high score!
