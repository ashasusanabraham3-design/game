HOW TO PLAY

1️⃣ Enter your name on the Home Screen.

2️⃣ Click START GAME.

3️⃣ Show both hands clearly to the camera.

4️⃣ Wait until your hands are detected.

5️⃣ 🤏 Make a Pinch Gesture (thumb + index finger) to START the game.


🎮 CONTROLS

🟦 Left Hand → Controls the BLUE saber.

🩷 Right Hand → Controls the PINK saber.

☝️ Move your index fingers to move both sabers.


📦 BLOCKS

📦 Blocks appear in the distance and move towards you.

➡️ Follow the arrow shown on the block

⬅️➡️⬆️⬇️ Swipe your finger in the SAME direction as the arrow

✅ Correct swipe = Score Point    ❌ Wrong swipe = No Point

🚫 Do NOT touch RED blocks    ❤️ Touching a RED block removes one life


🏆 Score as many points as possible and beat your high score!
